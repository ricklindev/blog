# Blog Cover

Before creating cover direction, read:

- `/DESIGN.md`
- `/docs/design-direction.md`
- `/references/typography-reference.png`
- this skill's `references/cover-rules.md`

## Workflow

1. Read the article or a reliable article summary.
2. Reduce the article to one visual idea or tension.
3. Choose one dominant subject.
4. Choose one ink from the approved palette unless directed otherwise.
5. Compose for a 3:2 landscape cover.
6. Keep 30–50% of the frame as active negative space.
7. Use a mechanical print treatment such as halftone, risograph, photocopy breakup, or restrained grain.
8. Generate original artwork only.
9. Keep the generated image text-free.
10. Check the quality gate.

## Quality gate

Before finalizing:

- Does the image communicate one idea?
- Is the paper visibly part of the composition?
- Is there meaningful negative space?
- Is one ink clearly dominant?
- Does it look printed rather than digitally glossy?
- Is the artwork original?
- Does it sit beside the approved prototypes without making the site feel like another brand?
- Is all typography left to HTML/CSS?
