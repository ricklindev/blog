# Cover Rules

## Canvas

- Preferred ratio: 3:2 landscape
- Flat front-facing artwork
- No device mockup, frame, desk, or cast shadow

## Substrate

Default to warm neutral paper compatible with the site background.

Paper exposure is part of the composition.

## Ink

Prefer one ink.

Approved starting palette:

- Cobalt `#2347B8`
- Terracotta `#C65F38`
- Botanical `#008A4B`
- Aubergine `#63365F`
- Charcoal `#30343A`

Do not permanently map colors to categories.

## Composition

- 30–50% active negative space
- one dominant subject
- asymmetrical editorial composition
- one focal event
- no evenly distributed decorative objects
- no centered generic poster template

## Treatment

Good:

- halftone
- risograph grain
- photocopy breakup
- clipped highlights
- paper knockouts
- restrained registration imperfection

Avoid:

- full-color stock photography with a tint
- glossy 3D
- cinematic lighting
- gradients
- decorative blobs
- scrapbook collage
- nostalgia for its own sake

## Typography

No text inside generated artwork.

Do not generate:

- title
- subtitle
- labels
- logo
- URL
- fake publication name
- QR code

Website typography is rendered by HTML/CSS.
