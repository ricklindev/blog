This is local sample content for the phase-one blog foundation. It exists to exercise a second published date and a different set of tags.

Some weeks look quiet because the useful work has not become visible yet. Reading carefully, revising a decision, and stepping away from a poor direction are part of the work too.

The calendar does not always show the difference, but a sustainable practice depends on making space for it.
